Who we are

A direct connection from farm to market

Farm to Mart (F2M) is a developing agricultural marketplace concept. Our aim is to help farmers present their produce and help buyers discover suppliers more easily. We are starting with fresh and semi-husked coconuts from Tamil Nadu.
